Annex A — What this contribution does not do
============================================

The limits are stated as a list, so that a reader can check them against the text
rather than infer them:

* It defines no encoding, schema, wire format or protocol.
* It does not replace the terminology work assigned to WG0; where this contribution
  uses a term, that use defers to the working group's definition.
* It does not address continuous remote attestation (WG1) or identity, behaviour and
  enforcement (WG2).
* It makes no claim of priority, novelty or absence of comparable work.
* It proposes no fields. The five questions in Chapter 2 are questions the record must
  answer, not columns the record must have.
* It does not seek a role, a seat, or an editorship. It is offered as text the working
  group may take, change or discard.

Annex B — Declared target
=========================

The declared target of this contribution is *no working group yet*: the subject is
advisory text for the cross-border recognition area (charter deliverable 3), and the
charter that would host it was still in draft when the contribution was prepared. If
the plenary reads the subject as belonging to an existing working group, the author
does not object, and asks only that the three requirements be read as written: as
requirements on what a record must answer, stated independently of any encoding.
